# A Note on the Second Edition

If you read the first edition of *Mission Built*, or an early copy of this one, welcome back. A few things changed.

Most visibly, the second edition adds a chapter: Chapter 13, *AI Is the New OS. The Mission Is the Only App That Matters.* It exists because 2026 forced the question. AI raised the floor of average work in a way no tool ever has, and the book's central argument, that mission, reps, feedback, recovery, and ownership are what produce real strength, needed to be tested against the loudest tool of the year. So I tested it, twice: once under the bar and once in the boardroom.

You'll notice a few other additions. The Drift, named once in the first edition, now recurs as a deliberate framework: Boeing's Drift in Chapter 1, the recovery-shaped Drift in Chapter 6, the legacy-shaped Drift in Chapter 12, and the AI-shaped Drift in Chapter 13. Three appearances of an idea is when it becomes a reader's framework, and four is when it becomes the spine of how you think about work.

The give-a-shit motto now has its source on the page. It belongs to Marine squadron VMM-364, the Purple Foxes, CASEVAC pilots who fly into enemy fire to bring wounded troops home, and it is three words on their patch. The motto came to me through Nate Fick at Endgame, and it has been the working motto of my career.

Some examples are paired with updates from 2024 to 2026: Boeing's 737 MAX now sits next to the 2024 Alaska Airlines door plug, and Cyberpunk 2077 next to the Humane AI Pin. The principle holds, and the evidence is current.

The second edition also got a rep it needed. The first version went out with prose that had been edited into fragments, and readers who know how I talk noticed. This revision restores the voice. The argument, the stories, and the sources did not change.

What didn't change is the mission and the give-a-shit. The voice is back where it belongs. Those are the work.

— Mike
